DUNGEON ESCAPE - PLAYER INSTRUCTIONS

GOAL
Guide your player (🧍) through the dungeon to the finish flag (🏁).
Collect the red key (🔴) to pass the red door (🟥), then collect the
blue key (🔵) to pass the blue door (🟦) on the way to the exit.
Reach the flag before you run out of moves or health.

STARTING A GAME
Run MyProgram in your Java environment using a terminal that displays emoji.
At the "how do you want to play?" prompt, enter one number and press Enter:
  1 - Human player: you choose each move.
  2 - Computer player: the computer plays automatically.

If you choose the computer, enter a difficulty and press Enter:
  1 - Easy: randomly chooses a legal neighboring tile.
  2 - Hard: chooses legal moves toward the next key, door, or exit.
Hard mode can revisit tiles and does not guarantee an escape.
After difficulty selection, no more input is needed for a computer game.

HOW TO MOVE
When "enter how to move" appears, type one letter and press Enter:
  W - Move up one tile.
  A - Move left one tile.
  S - Move down one tile.
  D - Move right one tile.
Uppercase and lowercase letters both work. Enter one move at a time,
without spaces. For example, enter D and press Enter to move right.
You cannot move diagonally, through walls, outside the board, or through
a colored door without its matching key. A blocked move leaves you in
place and costs no moves. An invalid letter also costs no moves; try again.

BOARD SYMBOLS AND ITEMS
  🧍 Player       Your current position.
  ⬜ Floor        An open tile you can enter.
  ⬛ Wall         Blocks movement.
  🔴 Red key      Pick up by entering its tile; unlocks the red door.
  🟥 Red door     Requires the red key to enter.
  🔵 Blue key     Pick up by entering its tile; unlocks the blue door.
  🟦 Blue door    Requires the blue key to enter.
  🧪 Potion       Restores 25 health when you enter its tile.
  ⚡ Speed item   Adds 5 moves when you enter its tile.
  🪤 Trap         Removes 20 health when you enter its tile.
  🏁 Finish flag  Enter this tile to escape and win.
  👾 Monster and 💰 treasure are displayed but have no effect in this version.

MOVES AND HEALTH
You start with 25 moves and 100 health. Every successful move costs one
move. The console displays the remaining move count before each turn.
The speed item adds 5 moves after the movement cost, for a net gain of 4
on that turn. Potion healing can raise health above 100 in this version.
Keys and tile effects activate automatically when you step onto them.
After you leave an item or trap tile, it becomes floor; its effect does
not repeat when you return.

WINNING AND LOSING
Win: enter the finish flag. The console says you escaped the dungeon.
Lose: your remaining moves reach zero, or your health reaches zero or less,
before you escape. The console prints the reason and the game ends.
Reaching the flag on your last move still counts as a win.
To play again, run MyProgram again and choose a player mode.

TIP
Plan your route before moving: backtracking uses your limited moves.
Watch for keys, avoid traps where possible, and use the speed item for
extra moves. The current 25-move limit makes the dungeon challenging;
these instructions describe the goal, not a guaranteed winning route.
